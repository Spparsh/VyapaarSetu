
# VyapaarSetu

### Bridging Business Intelligence with Growth.

**VyapaarSetu** is an AI-powered **Merchant Growth Manager** designed to help small and medium businesses turn their business data into actionable growth decisions.

Instead of simply showing analytics, VyapaarSetu identifies opportunities, explains them in simple language, recommends actions, and tracks the resulting business impact.

---

## Key Features

* **Business Intelligence** — Understand sales, customers and business trends.
* **AI Growth Manager** — Set business goals and receive actionable recommendations.
* **Opportunity Detection** — Identify customer, sales and campaign opportunities.
* **Personalized Actions** — Generate merchant-specific recommendations.
* **Merchant Approval** — Review, edit or approve AI-suggested actions.
* **Outcome Tracking** — Measure conversions, revenue and campaign performance.
* **Learning Loop** — Use previous outcomes to improve future recommendations.

---

## Core Workflow

**OBSERVE → UNDERSTAND → FIND OPPORTUNITY → RECOMMEND → APPROVE → EXECUTE → MEASURE → LEARN**

## Tech Stack

| Layer    | Technologies                                   |

---

## Architecture

```text
Merchant UI
     ↓
AI Orchestrator
     ↓
Business Intelligence & ML
     ↓
Merchant Data / Features
     ↓
Database & Data Services
     ↓
Action APIs
```

The architecture separates **deterministic analytics** from **AI reasoning**, allowing the AI layer to work with structured business insights instead of raw transaction data.
